After the fall, you feel that you are in a completely different place, you can no longer hear the pulsating beat, the air around you feels warmer, more welcoming. You look around, the cliff where you fell from is no longer there. In front of you lays a wooden door with some light coming from the small gaps between the frame.


You open the door the light coming from inside blinds you the sound deafens you. It takes a moment for you to gather your senses. As you readjust to the new reality you notice you are inside a tavern. People talking. mugs clashing, the fireplace cracking. You hear an old dwarf call for you -> [[Barkeeper]]
