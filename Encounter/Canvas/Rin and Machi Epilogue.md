Big pay required a big distraction, some of your comrades decided to lure the man eater bear into town, as he spread terror and horror through town you took the chance and went for the gold. Quick in quick out you thought, noone would ever know you were there, but something did take notice, the thing that allowed you in stopped your way out. You were cornered by the bear, he jumped on Machi and slashed him on his face, Rin quickly grabbed a nearby torch and threw it on the bear scaring of the beast and saving Machi from further wounds. As you tried to get back on your feet the guards coming, your luck had run dry, and they put you to jail.
  But your minds where not meant to be caged, as a guard comes to give you your daily meal, Rin bashed his head into the prison bars, grab the keys on his waist and make a run for freedom. But that was the easy part, you needed to escape the stronghold, and for that you needed the biggest distraction yet. You find the guards barracks filled with gunpowder, machi starts a small fire, hopping it would spread and make a run for it. You dont even dare to look back, time was against you, only stopping when your legs could no longer stand. As you fall on the hill, you look back and see the whole town in flames. You look at the destruction you caused. This was the last drop, you could no longer stand to cause any more bloodshed or ruin. You vow for a peaceful life, you spend the next two decades trying the normal life. You settle on a town, make friends with your neighbours, tend to your garden and sing along in the towns festivals. You found peace, until one day soldiers making their rounds in the nearby towns recognize the scar on machis face, you couldnt even react the next thing you remember is the nightmare you just unfolded.
