**Seraphim the lost** born a lord raised to be the successor a long line of kings. Trained from a young age by a [[Niels]], he spent a lot of time studying how to be a good king, but his mind did not care for politics or the glamorous life of a king, his heart sought adventure.

His father was a peaceful king, but many envied his power and did not agree with his ways. During a feast he was stabbed in the back from one of his men, bribed by one of his enemies, the true culprit was never found. The young prince had to fill his father shoes, filled with vengeance on his heart he started a campaign of conquest.

Battle after battle he kept going. His boldness grew after every victory, he went after stronger enemies, his bravery was commended by his kin but it was his undoing. One battle he rushed straight in the front lines, in the heat of battle he was separated from his allies. Many tried to find him but with no avail, they were being overwhelmed by the enemies and had to flee to avoid further losses. Assumed dead one of his father enemies assumed the throne.

Ashamed of letting his short temper get the best of him, he spent years honing his skills, trying to regain his honor and be worthy for the task he never wanted. On his journey he heard news about his kingdom,  learned one of his father's enemies was now king, and his greed and lack of compassion had made the kingdom fall to rot and decay. The time was nigh he started gathering men to regain his birth duty.

After gathering a small army, the assault began, the enemies morale was low and the young king made fast progress, the ecstasy of battle makes him lose his temper again, makes him feel invincible and he jumps to the front lines alone and is overwhelmed by the enemy.
